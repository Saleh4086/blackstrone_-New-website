Privacy Policy

Blackstone Signature Properties & Investments ("Blackstone," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how information submitted through our website and communications may be collected, used, and protected.

Information we collect

We may collect information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, mobile phone number, property or real-estate interests, repair or property-management information, and the contents of messages or forms you submit. We may also receive ordinary technical information generated when you visit the website.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, provide real estate and property-management services, coordinate appointments and property matters, communicate with customers, owners, tenants and vendors, manage requested services, maintain business records, and improve our website and customer service.

SMS and mobile information

If you expressly opt in to receive text messages, Blackstone may send messages related to your inquiry, requested services, appointments, real estate or property-management updates, repair and maintenance coordination, vendor bid requests, transaction updates, and customer service communications.

Mobile information, text messaging originator opt-in data, and SMS consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We may use service providers solely as necessary to deliver communications or provide services on our behalf, subject to appropriate protections.

Message frequency varies based on your interactions and requested services. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP to opt out of text messages or HELP for assistance.

Sharing and service providers

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to service providers that support our website, communications, property management, transaction, or business operations, or when required by law. We do not sell your personal information for third-party marketing.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. No method of electronic transmission or storage is guaranteed to be completely secure. We retain information as reasonably necessary for business, legal, and service purposes.

Your choices

You may choose not to provide optional information. For SMS, reply STOP to unsubscribe. For assistance, reply HELP or contact Blackstone using the information below.
