Meet Sal

Sal has worked in real estate since 2004 and is the Broker/Owner of Blackstone Signature Properties & Investments. His work is centered on clear communication and hands-on guidance. Whether a client is purchasing a first home, preparing a property for sale, evaluating an investment or looking for help managing a rental, the goal is to provide one experienced point of contact from strategy through execution.
